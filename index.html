Parcel Ghost

You are a courier who only exists while standing still. Move and you turn ghost: guards can't see you, but you can't touch parcels either. Stop for a beat to turn solid, and you automatically pick up, drop, or deliver.

  • Jade parcel → Jade dock
  • Ion parcel → Ion dock
  • Amber parcel → Amber dock

Win: dock all 3 before the 90s shift ends. Lose: a guard photographs you solid inside its cone, or any parcel is undelivered when the shift ends (carrying one doesn't count).

Move: WASD, arrows, stick, or drag the board. Stop to turn solid and interact. Esc / P pauses.
